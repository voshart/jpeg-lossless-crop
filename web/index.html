About cropping

The JPEG engine copies retained transform coefficients without re-encoding pixels. Turning may remove a thin edge strip after asking you. JPEG block-start edges align; opposite edges can end on any pixel.

Not a secure-redaction tool: boundary blocks can retain information outside the visible edge. Keeping original metadata can retain GPS coordinates and an uncropped thumbnail.

Controls

Hover to preview a JPEG block boundary. Drag toward the free-edge corner to refine the far corner by pixels; on an unrotated image, that is lower right. Drag inside to move or use a handle to resize. Arrow keys move a focused crop by JPEG blocks. A focused handle moves a constrained edge by blocks or a free edge by pixels; Shift moves 10 steps. Escape cancels a drag. Use Draw new to replace the selection. Zoom changes only the preview; use scrollbars to pan, or Fit to see the whole image.
